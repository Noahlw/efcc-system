## Problem Statement

<!-- efcc-roles-r3-ui-adoption:2026-10-28 -->
**Revision 3 · 28 October 2026 (Hong Kong).** Adopt the Owner-confirmed S3 UI/interaction contract in https://github.com/Noahlw/efcc-system/issues/194 (UI Revision1), including Q30/Q35 atomic Save shapes and Q32–Q34 confirmation/conflict/navigation behavior. #29 remains the business/authority specification; #184 remains delivery ownership. Prior Revision2 implementation/evidence under #191 is preserved at its original source and does not qualify these new requirements. This publication starts no new implementation, issue closure, merge or deployment.

EFCC's church administrators need to maintain fixed account roles and Department/Program responsibilities through the application. A local leader should be able to manage the Programs they are responsible for without receiving church-wide account-management authority. An administrator working across the church also needs a bounded way to find one person and maintain their permitted responsibilities.

The earlier Revision2 journey is present in the inspected candidate under #184 / PR #191, including Admin continuity and personal role history. Revision3 now adapts its presentation to the confirmed prototype and adds the explicitly agreed aggregate Save contracts. It is not an assertion that the existing candidate is merged/released or has already implemented or qualified this revision.

Changes must respect current authority, restrictions and independent responsibilities. Stale pages, concurrent requests, lost responses and shared-browser account switching must not preserve withdrawn access, remove a replacement assignment, disclose protected data or turn an uncertain outcome into a false success. Required role history must survive withdrawal and protect the existing account-deletion contract.

## Solution

Deliver phone-first Cantonese role management with two entry paths sharing one fixed assignment lifecycle:

- Department Managers start with a managed Department, select a Program, see its current Leaders and perform permitted grants or withdrawals.
- Admin and Role Assigner Staff use bounded person-first search for their authorized cross-scope work.

Keep the exclusive Global account role Member, Staff or Admin separate from Membership status and additive responsibilities. Preserve the existing actor/target matrix for Staff delegation, Department Managers and Program Leaders. Reuse current Department/Program records; assignments do not create Department membership or Program enrolment.

Add narrow in-app Admin continuity: a current eligible Admin can grant Admin directly to an eligible Member or Staff and remain Admin. Voluntary self-step-down is a separate password-confirmed action, explicitly choosing Member or Staff, defaulting to Member, and permitted only while another effective Admin remains. Entering or leaving Admin signs the changed account out of all Sessions. General forced demotion of another Admin remains excluded.

Add minimal recipient-only role-change events to the existing Inbox, including historical withdrawal notices. Keep those events separate from current authority, protected management receipts and independently authorized Staff/Admin Audit. Do not add an Own Responsibilities page or a general notification service.

This is Revision 3 of the existing #29 specification, historically Slice 3, incorporating the Owner-confirmed UI and atomic-save amendments of28 October2026 while retaining Revision2 history. It preserves O08/O09 except for the explicitly identified amendments and later single-Ban supersession recorded below. It is a specification publication, not implementation, qualification, full-v1 acceptance or release.

## User Stories

1. As an Admin, I want to assign Staff to an eligible Member or return Staff to Member, so that church-wide Staff authority matches current responsibility without changing Membership status.
2. As an Admin, I want to grant or withdraw Role Assigner delegation for eligible Staff, so that lower work-role administration can be delegated without granting Admin authority.
3. As an Admin, I want to manage Department Manager and Program Leader assignments for permitted targets, including Staff and Admin, so that protected accounts remain under Admin control.
4. As an Admin, I want to manage my own scoped assignments, so that my Department/Program responsibilities can be maintained independently of my Global account role.
5. As a Role Assigner Staff member, I want to grant or withdraw Department Manager and Program Leader responsibilities for permitted Member targets church-wide, so that local leadership can be maintained within my delegation.
6. As a Department Manager, I want to begin with a Department I manage and select one of its Programs, so that I can perform local leadership work in its actual context.
7. As a Department Manager, I want to see a Program's current Leaders and choose an eligible Member of that Department for a new grant, so that local delegation stays within my authority.
8. As a Department Manager, I want existing in-scope assignees to remain findable for permitted withdrawal after leaving the Department or becoming restricted, so that cleanup does not depend on current new-grant eligibility.
9. As an Admin or Role Assigner, I want bounded name/Username search, so that I can identify the right person for authorized cross-scope work without receiving a full account directory.
10. As a role manager, I want only the safe identity and assignment information my current authority permits, so that unrelated roles, contact data and recovery details remain private.
11. As a role manager, I want to review the explicit affected person or people, actions, fixed roles and scopes before submission, so that I understand the intended single change or permitted atomic set.
12. As a person holding several authorities, I want the permitted management tasks available through the existing navigation, so that I can work without switching to a separate role identity.
13. As an account holder, I want Global account role, Membership status, Department membership and Program enrolment to remain distinct, so that a role change does not silently change those relationships.
14. As an account holder with independent responsibilities, I want withdrawal of one grant to preserve access supplied by the others, so that unrelated work remains available.
15. As an Admin, I want ordinary new grants restricted to eligible accounts while permitted withdrawal remains possible for restricted targets, so that restrictions prevent new privileges without blocking cleanup.
16. As a restricted account holder, I want unwithdrawn assignments and history retained while their business access is suspended, so that clearing the applicable restrictions can restore only still-valid responsibilities.
17. As an Admin, I want leaving Staff to withdraw Role Assigner without later automatic restoration, so that a future Staff grant does not revive old delegation.
18. As an Admin, I want to grant Admin directly to an eligible Member or Staff, so that Admin continuity does not require an artificial intermediate Staff promotion.
19. As an Admin granting another Admin, I want the grant to take effect on my password-confirmed explicit submission, so that continuity does not depend on an invitation or recipient acceptance state.
20. As an Admin granting another Admin, I want to remain Admin and keep my own Session, so that adding a colleague is separate from relinquishing my own role.
21. As an Admin, I want voluntary self-step-down to explicitly offer Member or Staff with Member selected by default, so that the resulting Global account role is deliberate.
22. As an Admin considering self-step-down, I want the server to preserve another effective Admin under concurrency, so that simultaneous changes cannot leave the church without effective administration.
23. As an account holder entering or leaving Admin, I want all of my Sessions revoked and a fresh sign-in required, so that the privilege transition has the agreed Session boundary.
24. As an Admin stepping down, I want Membership and independent scoped responsibilities preserved without reviving old Role Assigner delegation, so that only the explicitly chosen Global-role transition occurs.
25. As a role manager, including a non-Staff Department Manager, I want sensitive submission bound to my current Session, credential and ten-minute password confirmation, so that expired or another account's proof cannot authorize it.
26. As an account holder, I want subsequent protected requests to reflect grants and withdrawals, including current Home notice visibility, so that cached pages cannot preserve withdrawn authority.
27. As a Program Leader, I want the authorized notice visibility for my Program without implicit enrolment or Department membership, so that the role supplies only its agreed business access.
28. As a role manager, I want a stale withdrawal to leave a replacement assignment intact, so that an old request cannot undo a newer grant without fresh review.
29. As a role manager with independent valid authority, I want an unrelated grant change not to invalidate an otherwise authorized operation, so that permission checks remain precise.
30. As an Admin, I want regranting withdrawn delegation not to revive obsolete pending intent, so that restoring authority does not silently execute an old request.
31. As a role manager, I want validation, denial, conflict, service failure and UNKNOWN distinguished, so that I can choose the appropriate correction, refresh or authorized retry.
32. As a role manager whose response was lost, I want permitted reconciliation or safe same-operation retry without duplicate effects, history, Audit or notices, so that network failure cannot create a second role change.
33. As an administrator, I want protected role-management results to require current applicable authority even for the original actor, so that an operation reference cannot preserve revoked access.
34. As an Admin whose self-step-down response was lost, I want honest UNKNOWN feedback and normal authorized navigation after fresh sign-in, so that the application does not invent success or add a post-revocation receipt exception.
35. As a person switching accounts on a shared browser, I want old protected role data and drafts discarded and late responses ignored, so that the new account cannot inspect or replay the previous person's work.
36. As a recipient of a committed role change, I want a minimal event in my existing Inbox, so that I can learn that my role was granted, changed or withdrawn.
37. As a former assignee, I want my historical event to retain its action, affected role, scope name at the time and event time under current Inbox eligibility, so that the notice remains meaningful after withdrawal without implying current authority.
38. As an eligible Inbox recipient, I want personal role notices to exclude other people's assignments, contact/recovery data and protected operation references, so that personal history does not become a management-data bypass.
39. As an authorized Staff/Admin Audit reader, I want meaningful role-change Audit retained under independent current Audit authority, so that investigation remains possible without reopening role-management result access.
40. As an administrator managing account lifecycle, I want retained Global-role transition and assignment history to block hard deletion after withdrawal, so that a former role cannot lose its required church-business history.
41. As an administrator managing otherwise-eligible deletion, I want application, approval, account-security or Approved Membership history alone to retain its existing treatment, so that the role-history guard does not turn into an unrelated blanket deletion ban.
42. As a phone or desktop user, I want accessible Cantonese controls and truthful loading, empty, denied, conflict, failure and uncertain states, so that I can finish the permitted journey without depending on future feature screens.

43. As a permitted role manager, I want one selected person’s explicit net Department Manager/Program Leader changes saved atomically while untouched/unloaded assignments remain unchanged, so that one Save cannot partially apply my reviewed set.
44. As a permitted Program manager, I want several explicitly selected eligible people granted leadership in that one Program atomically, so that one invalid target or required-write failure leaves the whole selection unchanged.

## Implementation Decisions

### D01 — Fixed role model and ownership

- Keep one exclusive Global account role: Member, Staff or Admin. Account classification does not approve Membership. Role Assigner is additive delegation available only to Staff; Department Manager and Program Leader are fixed scoped responsibilities.
- Keep credentials/Sessions with the existing authentication owner and church-business authorization with EFCC. Reuse existing confirmation, bounded-reader, command, Audit, receipt and presentation patterns through role-specific authority. Do not introduce a second session owner, generic permission editor or authority-history engine.
- Role assignment, Department membership, Program enrolment and Membership status have independent lifecycles. Preserve independently valid access rather than using a blanket invalidation rule.

### D02 — Preserved actor, target and scope matrix

Apply this matrix to public reads/writes and crafted requests. Current account eligibility and fresh confirmation are additional requirements.

| Current authority | Permitted changes | Target and scope limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | Member/Staff transitions; Role Assigner grant/withdrawal; Department Manager/Program Leader assignments; the narrow Admin continuity actions in D05 | May manage protected Staff/Admin targets' scoped assignments and own scoped assignments. No general forced demotion of another Admin. Sensitive account-maintenance self/Admin-target prohibitions are unchanged. |
| Role Assigner Staff | Department Manager/Program Leader assignments | Church-wide, permitted Member account targets only. No self-targeting, protected Staff/Admin management, Staff/Admin grant or further Role Assigner delegation. |
| Department Manager | Program Leader assignments | Programs currently belonging to managed Departments. New-grant candidates must be eligible Member accounts currently belonging to that Department. Existing in-scope assignees remain findable for permitted withdrawal after departure/restriction. No self-targeting or protected Staff/Admin management. |
| Staff or Program Leader alone | No role-management authority from that status alone | An independently held Department Manager assignment still supplies its permitted powers. Staff account tools and independently authorized Audit remain separate. |

Admin/Role Assigner's broader grant rules do not acquire the Department Manager's target Department-membership requirement. The narrow role-management self-change permissions do not broaden sensitive account Identity, credentials, restriction or deletion permissions.

### D03 — Hybrid entry and bounded projections

- Preserve the existing four top-level destinations: Home, Inbox, Manage and Account, with Manage exposed only for currently permitted delivered tasks. A non-Staff Department Manager can reach authorized local management. Staff identity alone does not add a role-management read entry.
- Department Managers use Department → Program → current Leaders → permitted grant/withdrawal. Admin/Role Assigner retain bounded person-first search for cross-scope work. Both paths share the fixed assignment lifecycle, review, proof and mutation rules.
- Use bounded name/Username search and bounded scope rosters. Apply current authority and purpose-specific projection at both page and API boundaries; selected-person/assignment reads require their own authorization independently of the current list page. A scope roster is not a full Department-member or account directory.
- Return only necessary safe identity fields and authorized assignment information. Admin may see authorized full role information; Role Assigner sees authorized church-wide scoped assignments; Department Manager sees Program Leader assignments within managed Programs. Exclude email, phone, recovery contacts and unrelated protected assignments. Preserve safe “Admin required” feedback for protected targets.
- Distinguish eligible new candidates from existing assignees needed for withdrawal. Review explicit affected person/action/role/scope before submission. D03a and D03b define exactly two Owner-approved bounded atomic mutation shapes: one Program/multiple-person grants and one person/multiple explicit scoped changes. Other bulk changes, role copying, role-switching identity and an Own Responsibilities summary/page remain excluded.

### D03a — Bounded same-Program multi-person grants (Owner amendment, 28 October 2026)

- The Owner confirmed selecting several eligible people for **one currently authorized Program**, then one explicit Save. This is grant-only; it does not authorize bulk withdrawal, multiple-Program person batches, a global directory or a select-all-search-results action. Existing actor/target/scope and ordinary-grant eligibility rules apply separately to every selected person. A Department Manager still requires each new candidate's current Department membership; Admin/Role Assigner do not inherit that extra condition.
- Candidate lists remain bounded. Retain explicit selections across search and paging only within this authorized Program/task lifetime, with minimal already-authorized name/Username identity and an accessible selected-only view. Reset clears choices; leaving/discarding or actor/context replacement clears their presentation. Recheck every selected target and current authority at the real mutation boundary; formerly visible identity is not authority.
- **All succeed or none writes.** Use one typed, bounded atomic command for the explicit target set, one original actor-bound operation key/fingerprint and one protected result. All required assignment effects, retained history, Audit, Inbox events and receipt succeed together or roll back together. Do not implement an atomic-looking Save by looping existing single-target endpoints. Before production, specify a finite request limit using the existing transport/native D1 budget and qualify the actual SQL-time all-target guards and required-write rollback. These API/storage contracts and limits are not yet implemented or qualified by the prototype.
- In the candidate list, a quiet 44px minimum toggle animates plus to minus, exposes a stable accessible label and aria-pressed state, and respects reduced motion. Minus cancels an unsaved selection; existing-grant withdrawal stays in the scoped editor. The Program header plus remains entry into the picker. Show the Program scope, selected identities and count; use the shared viewport-bottom Reset/Save layout. No duplicate ordinary change-summary block is required.
- Reuse valid confirmation. When confirmation is missing/expired, retain the selected set in the same authorized task, then require a fresh explicit Save after confirmation; never auto-submit. Pending blocks mutation. Known no-write preserves editable choices. Known conflict means the whole batch did not write: preserve a read-only previous-selection reference, refresh current authorized candidates, and require fresh selections/explicit Save. UNKNOWN claims neither success nor definite failure; reconcile or safely retry the same original operation, never create a replacement command. Preserve existing protected readback, account-switch and late-response rules.
- Prototype execution is synthetic only. This amendment records scope/behavior, does not start a production implementation stage, change an issue status, approve an artifact, merge, deploy or authorize remote-data work. D03b now records the distinct same-person/multiple-scoped-change contract; aggregate API/storage implementation and qualification for both shapes remain dependencies.

### D03b — One person, multiple explicit scoped changes (Owner Q30/Q34 amendment)

- One selected person's explicitly changed Department Manager/Program Leader assignments may be submitted as one bounded net-change set and one Save. A Program-scoped editor remains limited to its permitted Program. Account identity/Admin grant and Role Assigner retain independent command/save semantics; visual grouping does not combine them.
- Read and independently authorize the selected person and every editable current assignment status/witness. A catalog row or absence from a partial assignment page is UNKNOWN, never an unchecked/unassigned baseline. Withhold mutation until the touched baseline is known; preserve unchanged and unloaded assignments, and remove reverted choices from the request. No whole directory or unconditional full replacement is introduced.
- Use one original actor-bound operation key, canonical request fingerprint and aggregate command/receipt/reconciliation contract for the complete explicit set. Every required change, history, Audit, recipient Inbox event and receipt succeeds together or none writes. Apply current actor/target/eligibility/Program ownership and exact reviewed withdrawal-assignment witnesses at SQL execution, preserving independently valid authority and O09 protections. Do not loop existing single-target/scope endpoints or silently split one Save into committed chunks.
- Current valid proof is reused. On missing/expired confirmation, retain only the same still-authorized unsent task/choices, return after the existing Security protocol and require a new explicit Save. No permanent role-password field, automatic submission or rebinding of an already submitted original operation.
- A known no-write on the unchanged baseline may retain editable choices. A server-confirmed whole-batch conflict preserves prior intent only as an exceptional read-only reference, reads the latest authorized baseline, and requires fresh choices plus explicit Save. No automatic merge, rebind, partial retry or resubmit. UNKNOWN remains separate and resolves only the original submitted operation under current applicable authority.
- Choose and validate a finite request bound against the actual transport/native D1 budget before effects. Native batch rollback on SQL error alone is insufficient: a successful guard UPDATE affecting0 rows does not automatically abort. Required guard/effect failure must be represented by the existing aggregate SQL assertion/zero-effect gate so no partial business/required side effects can commit; qualify guard misses and injected failures at the real native write boundary. See https://github.com/Noahlw/efcc-system/issues/194 UI14 for documentation-checked implementation constraints.

### D04 — Eligibility, restrictions and independent scopes

- Ordinary new in-app grants retain the existing conditions: approved Active Membership, no Security Ban or deactivation, and no outstanding required first password change. Unverified email alone is not a new restriction. Do not extend the additional usable-permanent-credential Admin-target condition in D05 to every ordinary role grant.
- Authorized withdrawal remains possible for restricted or departed assignees where the actor/target matrix permits. Restrictions suspend relevant business access without deleting unwithdrawn assignments/history. Clearing restrictions restores only independently valid retained permissions; Unban does not reactivate Membership.
- Staff → Member and Staff → Admin withdraw Role Assigner with required history/Audit. Later Staff regrant does not restore it. Independent unwithdrawn Department/Program responsibilities remain.
- Resolve Program ownership from current server records. Department Manager powers follow the current owning Department and never confer another Department's management authority. Do not implement scope movement/archive policy here; those workflows retain their later feature owners.

### D05 — Narrow in-app Admin continuity

- An eligible current Admin can grant Admin directly to an eligible Member or Staff. The target requires approved Active Membership, no Security Ban, a usable permanent credential and no outstanding required first password change. No intermediate Staff role is required.
- The grant activates on the actor's explicit submit with valid current password confirmation. The grantor remains Admin. There is no recipient invitation, acceptance or activation state.
- Voluntary self-step-down is a separate explicit password-confirmed action. Offer Member or Staff, default Member; do not silently choose based on a previous role. Preserve Membership and independent scoped assignments. Old Role Assigner never revives automatically.
- Permit self-step-down only if another effective Admin remains. Effective Admin means Active Membership, no Ban and usable permanent credentials; it is not a count of live Sessions. Preserve this invariant at the actual write boundary under concurrent requests and relevant state changes; no temporarily unprotected handover.
- This expressly supersedes Revision 1/O08/O09's D1-only/no-in-app-transition clauses only for adding another Admin and voluntary self-step-down. General forced peer Admin demotion, first-Admin bootstrap, Admin-target recovery, emergency operator execution and production SQL remain outside this specification. Preserve applicable database defenses and invariants for separately authorized direct-D1 maintenance; do not build an operator service or imply its operational qualification.

### D06 — Session and confirmation boundary

- Entering or leaving Admin revokes all Sessions of the changed account as a required part of the committed change; fresh sign-in is required. Self-step-down includes the initiating Session. Granting Admin to another person does not revoke the grantor's own Session.
- Reuse the current Session/credential-bound ten-minute password confirmation for sensitive role changes, including eligible Department Managers who are not Staff. Confirmation is not transferable across Sessions, credential revisions or accounts.
- Do not add a permanent extra role-password field or a second confirmation protocol. Reuse valid existing proof; missing/expired proof returns through the existing Security-owned confirmation form to the same still-authorized unsent choices and requires explicit Save. Password-confirmation expiry is distinct from authentication Session expiry; neither permits automatic submission or cross-account reuse.
- Check current Session, credentials, actor/target eligibility, authority, scope, applicable grant and proof at the actual mutation boundary. Reuse existing origin, rate-limit, private/no-store and safe-error protections. Current authority governs protected reads as well as writes.

### D07 — Atomic changes, replay and stale intent

- A successful command commits its one reviewed mutation unit: a single change or exactly one of the bounded explicit D03a/D03b sets, with all required retained role history, meaningful Audit and an immutable aggregate operation receipt. Include required target Session revocation, delegation cleanup and recipient Inbox events in the same atomic consistency boundary. Injected failure of a required write must roll the change back without partial effects or success notices.
- Retained records identify actor, time, target, role/action and relevant scope without depending on continued live identity/assignment rows. Preserve the difference between deduplicated business effects/Audit and request/security-attempt diagnostics.
- Bind the original actor, target/action, operation key and fingerprint. Authorized replay or reconciliation of that operation must not duplicate effective assignments, history, corresponding business Audit or Inbox events. Duplicate/conflicting/currently obsolete submissions cannot silently become a different operation.
- A stale withdrawal must not remove an assignment that replaced the reviewed withdrawn/regranted assignment; require fresh authoritative state and explicit review of the replacement. Regranting revoked delegation must not resurrect intent that relied on the withdrawn grant.
- Conversely, changing one grant does not automatically invalidate an operation still covered by another independently valid authority source and all current actor/target/scope/eligibility conditions. No blanket re-review or generic permission-version framework is required.

### D08 — UNKNOWN, protected results and account switching

- Preserve O09-A–D. A timeout or lost response is UNKNOWN. Current-authorized reconciliation or safe explicit same-operation retry may settle it; no automatic replacement/recreation, blind resubmission, request cancellation or cross-account settlement is added.
- Protected role-management result/receipt lookup always requires current applicable management authority, including for the original actor possessing the original operation key. Independent Audit authority and personal Inbox history are not role-operation settlement exceptions. Permission denial or absence of a receipt does not prove success or failure.
- If self-step-down commits but its response is lost, Session revocation and lost Admin authority do not grant a special result reader. After fresh sign-in, normal authorized navigation and eligible personal Inbox events may orient the person; event presence or absence does not certify the exact original command. Preserve UNKNOWN where exact settlement is unavailable.
- Allow dismissal of transient local feedback without implying cancellation, success or failure. No own-role summary, Account recovery card, recovery dashboard, central operation queue or cross-device recovery service is introduced.
- On logout/account change, discard protected person/role/scope labels, results, draft choices and transient task state. Retain only minimal opaque original-operation reference plus original actor identity where existing recovery rules allow. Another account cannot inspect or replay it. Late responses must not repopulate a disposed task/account lifetime.

### D09 — Minimal recipient-only Inbox history

- Add role-change events to the existing Inbox for committed grants/withdrawals, including Global-role transitions and required Role Assigner withdrawal. Keep existing application-decision messages valid and distinct. Reuse the recipient-bound bounded Inbox read/presentation pattern; do not expose management Audit or receipts as notification data.
- A personal event contains only its change/action, the recipient's affected role, relevant scope name at the time and event time. A Global-role transition identifies the resulting Global role. Do not add other people's assignments, contact/recovery data or protected operation references.
- Retain the recipient's own historical event after withdrawal and allow reads under current Inbox eligibility. Preserve the scope name at the time rather than rewriting history from a current label. Historical events do not prove present authority or the exact outcome of a particular uncertain command.
- Ban, Deactivated/Security-only and other restricted states receive no new Inbox bypass. Committed-event delivery and same-operation deduplication are required; denied or failed attempts do not produce success notices.
- This is the explicit Q8/Q10 amendment to Revision 1's no-role-notification exclusion. Q9's proposed Own Responsibilities page was declined. No email, recipient acceptance, general notification hub/service, or unrelated notification feature is introduced.

### D10 — Audit, common role history and eligible deletion

- Preserve independently authorized read-only Staff/Admin Audit. Losing Role Assigner while retaining eligible Staff status does not remove that independent Audit entitlement. A former Department Manager who is only a Member does not gain it. Audit is not a role-receipt reconciliation bypass.
- Preserve all four existing historical church-business deletion blockers: enrolment, invitation, Department membership and Department Manager assignment. These are history-existence checks, not only current active relationships.
- Extend the shared retained-role-history protection to Staff and Admin Global-role transitions, Role Assigner delegation and scoped Department Manager/Program Leader history, including withdrawn/demoted roles. Member → Admin → Member with no other business history is a case of this common guard. There is no separate former-Admin deletion rule, Admin-history subsystem or policy engine; the separate Q11 proposal was withdrawn.
- Extend both public deletion eligibility and the actual database guard. Do not cascade away required history to permit deletion. Preserve durable Audit, immutable receipts and permanent Username reservation; deleted accounts lose credentials/Sessions and disappear from live account projections.
- Otherwise-eligible deletion with only application, approval, account-security or Approved Membership history retains its existing treatment. Initial Member creation/approval or arbitrary Audit rows do not become new role-history blockers merely by being history. Preserve existing operation-specific actor/target authority, races, current-state assertions and truthful history-blocked/denied outcomes without protected-data leakage.
- Apply the later single-Ban decision: no separate Account Security Lock model. A currently Banned target cannot be hard-deleted even without history blockers; verified Unban is required before a new deletion attempt and clears only the Ban hold. Other restrictions/history still apply. Preserve the separately owned S1 Ban/deletion/verified-recovery obligations and its evidence gate; do not claim they are implemented or absorb the full S1 programme into Roles. O09-E's proposed blanket Lock extension remains excluded.

## Testing Decisions

### T01 — Existing real public application seam

Use the existing real local Cloudflare Worker, disposable D1 and browser/public HTTP journey as the primary seam. Prefer observable behavior at that boundary over helper structure, schema-shape assertions or mocked authorization. Use direct public requests for cases the UI prevents. Reuse Native fixture, immutable-candidate, causal/failure and presentation owners; no separate acceptance application or new runtime infrastructure is justified by this spec.

Exercise role management, current Session/confirmation, bounded projections, Inbox, existing Home reads, independent Audit and account-deletion protection as connected behavior. Schema/race/global-state cases use the existing isolated-app capability; causal tests must reach the actual application write boundary.

### T02 — Delegation, scope and projection matrix

- Complete Admin → Role Assigner Staff → Department Manager → Program Leader delegation, then permitted withdrawal through both person-first and scope-first paths.
- Cover Admin protected-target/own-scoped changes; RA's Member-only church-wide scope; DM's current owning Department and new-candidate Department membership.
